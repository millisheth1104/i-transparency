## 2026-08-11 — Bestsellers card alignment: white gap under "Knitted Cable Knit Throw"

- Not a CSS bug: DOM geometry was already pixel-perfect (frame `getBoundingClientRect()` exactly matched its container, `object-fit:cover` correctly applied). The featured image I'd set for this product's "Green Mel" variant (`KNITSCAPE-TH-K3800101-D-1.jpg`) is a studio product shot with a plain white background margin baked into the photo itself. Because that photo's aspect ratio is wider than the 3:4 card, `object-fit:cover` crops its *left/right* excess to fit — but has nothing to crop *vertically*, so the white margin at the bottom of the source photo survives the crop and shows as a visible gap.
- Fix: swapped the featured image to a different photo already in that same product's media library — `KNITSCAPE-TH-K3800101-D-2.jpg`, a full-bleed lifestyle shot (throw draped over a couch, no white margin) — via `productReorderMedia`. - **Lesson for future image swaps**: before picking a product photo for a cropped card slot, check whether the source image has letterboxing/margin baked in — `object-fit:cover` only crops the dimension that has excess relative to the target aspect ratio; a margin on the *other* axis will always show through no matter how correct the CSS is.
